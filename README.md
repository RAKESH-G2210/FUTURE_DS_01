# FUTURE_DS_01 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Task Overview
This project is completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1.
The objective is to analyze business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products,
high-value categories, and regional performance.

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- CSV Sales Dataset

## Analysis Performed
- Revenue trend analysis over time
- Top 10 products by sales
- Category-wise sales and profit analysis
- Regional sales performance analysis
- KPI tracking (Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin)

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached $2.29M with a profit margin of 12.47%
- Sales show a steady upward trend over time
- Top products contribute a significant share of revenue
- Technology and Office Supplies categories perform strongly
- Regional performance varies in profitability

## Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing products and categories
- Improve pricing or operations in low-profit regions
- Use dashboards for continuous business monitoring
